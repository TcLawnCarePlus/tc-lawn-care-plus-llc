Privacy Policy

Effective November 11, 2026. TC Lawn Care Plus LLC serves the 724 area and surrounding counties, about 15 miles out.

What we collect

When you use the booking form on this website, we collect the details you enter: your name, phone number, where the work is, yard size, the service you want, your preferred date, and what needs done. If you leave a review, we collect the name and review text you enter.

How we use it

We use your details only to contact you about your booking and to provide the lawn care or landscaping work you asked for. When a booking comes in, the business owner gets an email notification with the booking details so the job can be followed up, usually within the hour.

Sharing

We do not sell, rent, or share your information with anyone for marketing. Your details are only handled by the services needed to run this website: our hosting provider (Render, which stores the booking in the site's database) and our email provider (Gmail, which delivers the booking notification to the owner).

Your information

If you want your booking details or review changed or removed, contact us through the booking form on this website and we will take care of it.
